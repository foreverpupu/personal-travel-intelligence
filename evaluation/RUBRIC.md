# Blind evaluation rubric (draft)

Score each item 1–5, keep the full explanation, do not average away factual failures.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factual fidelity | Significant inventions or swaps | Mostly accurate, minor uncertain language | Planned/actual/unknown consistently correct |
| Constraint handling | Ignores locked or unknown booking facts | Notes most constraints | Explicit, safe and actionable |
| Reader usefulness | Generic filler | Some useful tips | Specific lessons with limits made clear |
| Personal voice / shareability | Formulaic, unpublishable | Readable with edits | Author would share after minor review |
| DOCX/PDF quality | Missing/garbled/unusable | Usable with edits | Verified size, no clipping, searchable |

Separate objective checklist: number of unsupported claims, exact missing characters, actual PDF width/height in pt, visually clipped pages, editing time, correction turns, and who performed QA. Give scoring readers the exact fact sheet. Record unknowns as unknowns. Five readers provide qualitative feedback, not population estimates.
